**The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind**

“When the world is crumbling, some people pray. Others build.”
— William Kamkwamba
**Overview**
The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind is a 2019 British drama film directed by Chiwetel Ejiofor, based on the true story of William Kamkwamba, a young Malawian boy who built a wind turbine to save his village from famine.
The film is adapted from the memoir The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind: Creating Currents of Electricity and Hope by William Kamkwamba and Bryan Mealer.

**Main Characters**
William Kamkwamba – A curious and brilliant teenager who believes in the power of science.
Trywell Kamkwamba – William’s father, a hardworking farmer with traditional values.
Agnes Kamkwamba – His supportive and loving mother.
Chief Wimbe – The village leader balancing politics and survival.

**Plot Summary**
Amid a devastating drought in Malawi, William Kamkwamba faces hunger, poverty, and expulsion from school. Refusing to give in to despair, he scavenges electronic scraps and designs a windmill to pump water for crops.
What begins as a boy’s wild idea becomes a symbol of hope for an entire community.

**Key Themes**
Innovation through Adversity – The courage to build when resources are nonexistent.
Education as Empowerment – Knowledge becomes the weapon against poverty.
Tradition vs. Change – The tension between old ways and modern progress.
Perseverance – A reminder that belief and effort can turn scarcity into abundance.

**Why It’s Inspiring**
It champions curiosity, resilience, and faith in human potential.
It’s a cinematic reminder that real change begins with imagination.
The story bridges engineering, sacrifice, and hope into one unforgettable narrative.
